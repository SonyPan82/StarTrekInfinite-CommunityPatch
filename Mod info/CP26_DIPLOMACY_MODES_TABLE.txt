CP26 Diplomacy and Subject Systems
Date: 2026-05-06

Purpose
- Track the CP26 diplomacy changes in plain language.
- Explain what the game-start diplomacy option does.
- Record what was discovered while testing Patronage/Patronat.
- Document the new vassalization and occupation war flows.

Regular empires means:
- country_type = default
- country_type = contender

============================================================
PATRONAGE / PATRONAT DISCOVERY
============================================================
What we found
- The action named action_patronage is a real diplomacy action slot used by the engine.
- It appears to be tied to the game's subject/support-power diplomacy layer.
- When we tried to repurpose it directly for vassalization, the option could appear, but the confirmation flow was unreliable: the confirm button could stay greyed out or disappear.

Probable original intent
- Patronage looks like it was meant for a softer diplomatic relationship, closer to protection, sponsorship, or support between powers.
- It was probably not designed as a direct "make this empire my vassal" button.
- Because of that, using it for hard vassalization caused UI and confirmation problems.

Current CP26 decision
- Keep action_patronage hidden/unused for vassalization.
- Use a visible vanilla-compatible action slot for the vassalization proposal instead.
- Handle acceptance/refusal through CP26 events, where we control the result safely.

============================================================
VASSALIZATION DIPLOMACY
============================================================
Player action
- Action file: common/diplomatic_actions/00_hornblower_actions.txt
- Event file: events/zz_cp26_vassalization_events.txt

Flow
- The actor proposes vassalization.
- The target receives a delayed proposal event.
- If accepted, the target becomes a vassal.
- If refused, the actor receives a temporary Subjugation Casus Belli.
- The actor gets a compact diplomatic popup for accepted/refused results.

Galaxy notification
- Hook: common/on_actions/zz_cp26_vassalization_on_actions.txt
- When any empire becomes a subject, CP26 checks if the player knows both sides.
- If the player knows the new subject and its overlord, a compact diplomatic popup is shown.

SUBJUGATION WAR
============================================================
Casus Belli
- cb_subjugation
- File: common/casus_belli/zz_cp26_contender_casus_belli.txt

War Goal
- wg_subjugation
- File: common/war_goals/zz_cp26_subjugation_war_goals.txt

Flow
- Demand Surrender is available through the normal peace UI.
- If the enemy accepts or fully loses, it becomes a vassal.
- Status quo can create/keep a partial vassal outcome if the engine creates a released empire from occupied systems.
- The AI accepts/refuses surrender through the normal war-score and surrender-acceptance logic.

============================================================
NEW OCCUPATION WAR
============================================================
Casus Belli
- cb_cp26_forced_occupation
- File: common/casus_belli/zz_cp26_contender_casus_belli.txt

War Goal
- wg_cp26_forced_occupation
- File: common/war_goals/zz_cp26_forced_occupation_war_goals.txt

Intent
- Provide an always-available occupation-style war goal for regular independent empires.
- This is separate from the vanilla Cardassian wg_occupation.
- It does not overwrite the Bajoran/Cardassian occupation setup.

Flow
- The war goal is available whenever the CP26 occupation CB is valid.
- On war start, CP26 adds war claims on the target's systems so status quo can use the normal occupied-claims transfer logic.
- Status quo: the attacker may keep occupied claimed systems.
- Full victory: the target becomes an occupied_state subject, like the Bajoran/Cardassian occupation model.
- Attacker defeat/surrender: the occupation effect does not fire.

Important limitation
- The "keep conquered systems on status quo" behavior depends on the engine's claim/occupation peace logic.
- CP26 prepares the claims automatically, but final transfer is still resolved by the game's normal peace system.
