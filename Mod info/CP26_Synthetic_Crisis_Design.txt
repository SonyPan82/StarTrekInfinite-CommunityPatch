CP26 Synthetic Crisis Design
Star Trek: Infinite - Community Patch 2026

Purpose
-------
Create an optional narrative and gameplay system around synthetic workers in the
Federation, inspired by Star Trek: Picard season 1.

The goal is not to add generic Stellaris robots, but to create a Star Trek
system about industrial dependence, synthetic rights, fear, sabotage, Mars,
Utopia Planitia, and the political consequences of artificial life.

The system should be optional and player-driven. The Picard-style crisis should
not be forced in every campaign. It should emerge from research choices,
planetary decisions, and accumulated risk.


Core Fantasy
------------
The Federation can develop and deploy A500-style synthetic laborers to support
shipbuilding and industrial expansion, especially around Mars and Utopia
Planitia.

At first, the program is useful and rational:

- Faster ship construction
- Stronger industrial output
- Engineering research bonuses
- Better recovery from workforce shortages

Over time, the Federation must answer a larger question:

Are synths tools, workers, citizens, or people?

The answer changes the future of the Federation.


Main System Variables
---------------------
These variables should drive the arc.

cp26_synth_dependency
    Represents how much the Federation depends on synthetic labor.
    Increases when synths are deployed or industrial expansion is accelerated.
    High dependency increases industrial power but raises crisis risk.

cp26_synth_security
    Represents Starfleet and Daystrom safeguards.
    Increases through security choices, inspections, and restrictions.
    High security lowers crisis risk but reduces economic output.

cp26_synth_rights
    Represents the political and ethical recognition of synthetic life.
    Increases through reformist choices and rights-focused events.
    High rights lowers unrest and long-term danger but can reduce immediate
    industrial efficiency.

cp26_zhat_vash_infiltration
    Represents hidden sabotage and anti-synthetic manipulation.
    Increases slowly if Romulan actors exist, if security is low, or if the
    Federation ignores suspicious incidents.
    High infiltration can trigger the Mars Crisis.


Phase 1 - Technology
--------------------
Technology:

tech_cp26_synthetic_labor_program
    Name: Synthetic Labor Program
    Faction: Federation only at first
    Department: Engineering or Society
    Role: Unlocks the synthetic labor arc

Possible prerequisites:

- Industrial or engineering tech
- Mars / Luna / Sol exists
- Utopia Planitia or Federation shipbuilding infrastructure exists
- Year threshold if needed for pacing

Effects:

- Unlocks planetary decision: Deploy Synthetic Workforce
- Starts the Synthetic Question event chain
- Adds first small amount of cp26_synth_dependency


Phase 2 - Synthetic Pops
------------------------
Create a scripted synthetic species:

Species name:
    A500 Synth

Suggested traits:

trait_cp26_synthetic_laborer
    Industrial output bonus
    Reduced political weight
    No natural growth
    Cannot migrate freely at first

trait_cp26_positronic_limited
    Engineering output bonus
    Reduced happiness or amenities usage
    Represents early limited synthetic cognition

Implementation options:

Option A - True synthetic species
    Create an actual species by event and spawn A500 pops.
    More immersive, but requires careful testing.

Option B - Organic placeholder with synthetic trait
    Use a custom species class/portrait if possible, but avoid full robot rules.
    Safer for Star Trek: Infinite if robot systems are unstable or hidden.

Option C - Planet modifier only for version 1
    No actual pops at first. Use modifiers and events to simulate synth labor.
    Very safe, but less satisfying.

Recommended version 1:
    Use true or placeholder A500 pops only after testing that pop creation,
    jobs, rights, and migration do not break the economy.


Phase 3 - Deployment
--------------------
Planetary decision:

decision_cp26_deploy_synthetic_workforce
    Name: Deploy Synthetic Workforce

Available on:

- Mars
- Luna
- Sol industrial worlds
- Federation industrial colonies
- Worlds with shipyard, forge, industrial district, or major construction site

Effects:

- Creates 1 to 3 A500 Synth pops
- Adds local modifier: Synthetic Workforce
- Increases cp26_synth_dependency
- May reduce stability slightly
- May increase cp26_zhat_vash_infiltration if security is weak

Local modifier:

modifier_cp26_synthetic_workforce
    Ship build speed bonus
    Engineering research bonus
    Industrial output bonus
    Small stability penalty


Phase 4 - Tension Events
------------------------
After synths are deployed, random events begin.

Event examples:

1. Routine Deviation
    A minor behavioral anomaly is detected in an A500 unit.

    Choices:
    - Run full diagnostics: +security, -output
    - Ignore it: +dependency, +infiltration
    - Ask Daystrom to investigate: +rights, +security

2. Worker Protest
    Organic workers on Mars protest synthetic replacement.

    Choices:
    - Protect organic labor: -dependency, +stability, -output
    - Expand synthetic labor: +dependency, -stability, +output
    - Create mixed labor protections: +rights, +stability

3. Daystrom Petition
    Researchers argue the A500s show signs of emergent self-awareness.

    Choices:
    - Approve limited study: +rights, +engineering
    - Shut it down: +security, -rights
    - Commercialize the breakthrough: +dependency, +risk

4. Security Audit
    Starfleet Security requests deeper access to synthetic command routines.

    Choices:
    - Grant full audit: +security, -output
    - Limited audit: small +security
    - Deny request: +dependency, +infiltration

5. Romulan Warning
    A Romulan source warns that synthetic life is more dangerous than it seems.

    Choices:
    - Treat it as propaganda: +infiltration
    - Investigate quietly: +security
    - Open diplomatic inquiry: +rights, diplomacy tension

6. The Synthetic Question
    Federation Council debate over whether synths are equipment or persons.

    Choices:
    - Tools of industry: +dependency, -rights
    - Protected workers: +rights, +stability
    - Dangerous technology: +security, possible ban path


Phase 5 - Mars Crisis
---------------------
The Mars Crisis should be possible, not guaranteed.

Possible trigger conditions:

- Federation exists
- Mars or Utopia Planitia exists
- Synthetic Labor Program researched
- At least one A500 deployment happened
- cp26_synth_dependency is high
- cp26_synth_security is low, or cp26_zhat_vash_infiltration is high
- Optional year pacing around 2384-2385 equivalent

Major event:

cp26_synth.1000 - First Contact Day Incident

Description:
    On First Contact Day, Starfleet receives reports of synchronized anomalies
    across A500 labor units at Mars and Utopia Planitia. Security systems begin
    responding to unauthorized commands. The Federation has minutes to act.

Choices:

1. Cut the network immediately
    Requires decent security.
    High chance to contain the crisis.
    Destroys some synth pops.
    Adds temporary production penalty.

2. Save Utopia Planitia first
    Attempts to preserve shipbuilding infrastructure.
    Risk depends on security and infiltration.
    Can prevent total disaster but may increase casualties.

3. Blame the synthetics
    Moves toward Synth Ban.
    Reduces future risk.
    Large ethical and diplomatic consequences.

4. Launch an investigation
    Requires high rights or high security.
    Can reveal outside manipulation.
    Risky if infiltration is already too high.

5. Militarize Mars
    Strong control response.
    Preserves some industry.
    Increases authoritarian pressure and unrest.


Mars Crisis Outcomes
--------------------
Outcome A - Crisis Contained
    Mars survives.
    Some synth pops are shut down.
    Federation gains security lessons.
    Synthetic program continues under restrictions.

Outcome B - Partial Attack
    Utopia Planitia is damaged.
    Mars receives a strong negative modifier.
    Shipbuilding is reduced for several years.
    Federation must choose ban, reform, or militarized control.

Outcome C - Attack on Mars
    Major catastrophe.
    Mars is devastated or heavily modified.
    Utopia Planitia destroyed or disabled.
    Synthetic life is banned.
    Exploration and shipbuilding penalties.
    Possible Picard/Raffi narrative hooks later.

Outcome D - Conspiracy Exposed
    The Federation discovers outside manipulation.
    Zhat Vash / anti-synth infiltration is exposed.
    Synth ban can be avoided.
    Romulan diplomatic consequences.
    Synthetic rights path opens.


Phase 6 - Aftermath Paths
-------------------------
Path 1 - Synth Ban
------------------
The Federation bans synthetic lifeforms.

Effects:

- Disable Deploy Synthetic Workforce
- Remove or deactivate A500 pops
- Add country modifier: Synthetic Ban
- Reduce shipbuilding and engineering output
- Increase security and political stability in the short term
- Reduce Federation idealism or unity in the long term

Possible modifier:

modifier_cp26_synth_ban
    Ship build speed penalty
    Engineering research penalty
    Stability bonus
    Intel/security bonus


Path 2 - Synthetic Rights Accord
--------------------------------
The Federation recognizes synthetic personhood or protected legal status.

Effects:

- A500 pops become protected workers or citizens
- Synthetic unrest and crisis risk drop
- Engineering and unity bonuses
- Immediate industrial output lower than forced labor path
- Diplomatic tension with anti-synth factions

Possible modifier:

modifier_cp26_synthetic_rights_accord
    Engineering research bonus
    Stability bonus
    Unity bonus
    Lower industrial exploitation bonus


Path 3 - Militarized Control
----------------------------
The Federation keeps synth labor but under strict command restrictions.

Effects:

- Synthetic deployment remains available
- Strong output continues
- Stability penalty
- Rights stay low
- Future crisis can return if infiltration is not solved

Possible modifier:

modifier_cp26_synth_control_protocols
    Industrial output bonus
    Ship build speed bonus
    Stability penalty
    Espionage/security upkeep cost


Path 4 - Daystrom Reform
------------------------
A middle path focused on science, ethics, and oversight.

Effects:

- Synthetic labor continues on limited worlds
- Crisis risk reduced
- Research bonus
- Slower deployment
- Opens future positronic technology


Integration with Existing CP26 Content
--------------------------------------
Mars and Luna mod content:
    Mars should be the primary location for the Synthetic Labor Program.
    If Mars has CP26 modifiers, the synth arc should interact with them.

Utopia Planitia:
    If represented by building, modifier, starbase, or shipyard, it should gain
    special bonuses and crisis vulnerability.

Romulan Supernova:
    The Mars Crisis can be linked to the Romulan rescue effort.
    More rescue fleet preparation means more synth dependency and higher stakes.

Picard-era narrative:
    Optional later hooks:
    - Picard resignation
    - Raffi dismissal
    - Daystrom restrictions
    - Synthetic ban repeal
    - Zhat Vash exposure

No Pirates option:
    No direct interaction required.

Added civilizations:
    Romulan-adjacent powers may react differently to synthetic research.
    Tholians, Breen, Gorn, and Orion should probably not use the system in v1.

Version 1 Scope
---------------
Keep version 1 focused and safe.

Implement:

- 1 technology
- 1 synthetic species or simulated pop system
- 1 planetary decision
- 4 variables
- 6 tension events
- 1 Mars Crisis event
- 3 aftermath paths: Ban, Rights Accord, Militarized Control
- English and French localization

Avoid in version 1:

- Full robot empire mechanics
- Complex species rights rewrites
- AI use of synth deployment
- Huge economy rebalance
- Forced canon timeline for every game


Design Principles
-----------------
1. Optional
    The player should choose to enter the synth arc.

2. Star Trek first
    The system should be about ethics, politics, and consequences, not only
    production bonuses.

3. Risk should be readable
    The player should understand that dependency without security is dangerous.

4. Multiple valid endings
    Ban, reform, and rights should all be playable.

5. Compatible with CP26
    The system must avoid breaking startup scripts, added civilizations, or
    existing Mars/Luna content.


Open Questions
--------------
1. Should synth pops be real pops in version 1, or simulated by modifiers first?

2. Should the Mars Crisis be tied to the year 2385, or to player progression?

3. Should the Romulans/Zhat Vash be explicitly named, or kept as hidden
   infiltration until late events?

4. Should the Synth Ban be a Federation-only policy, or a broader galactic
   diplomatic action?

5. Should Mars be permanently damaged in the worst outcome, or recoverable via
   a long reconstruction project?


Recommended First Implementation
--------------------------------
Start with a safe version:

1. Add the technology.
2. Add the planetary decision on Mars and industrial Federation worlds.
3. Use planet modifiers first.
4. Track dependency, security, rights, and infiltration.
5. Add tension events.
6. Add the Mars Crisis.
7. Only after this is stable, add true A500 pops.

This keeps the first release stable while preserving the full design direction.
