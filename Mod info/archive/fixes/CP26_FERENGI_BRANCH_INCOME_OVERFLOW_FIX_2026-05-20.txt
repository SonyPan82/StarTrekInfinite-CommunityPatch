CP26 Ferengi Branches - Income Overflow Fix
Date: 2026-05-20

Issue
- A single branch office could generate absurd energy income spikes (example: +7K).

Root cause
- The branch income tier script used invalid `check_variable` comparisons with two `value` clauses.
- In practice, the engine could evaluate many tier blocks as valid at once.
- This caused one branch to receive multiple stacked payout tiers instead of exactly one.

Fix
1. Exact tier matching
- Replaced broad/invalid tier checks with exact equality checks:
  - trade count = 1..23
  - mining count = 1..23
  - energy count = 1..23
- Implemented in:
  common/scripted_effects/cp26_ferengi_branch_income_effects.txt

2. Safer energy stat
- Replaced `country_base_energy_produces_add` with `country_energy_produces_add`
  for branch-energy payout modifiers.
- Implemented in:
  common/static_modifiers/cp26_ferengi_branch_modifiers.txt

Expected behavior
- 1 Bureau du Grand Nagus = +12 energy
- 1 Equipe d'Acquisition = +12 minerals
- 1 Bar de Quark = +16 energy
- No multi-tier overflow from a single branch.

Testing note
- On an existing save, allow at least one monthly pulse so the branch backend can
  clear and rebuild the active income modifiers.
