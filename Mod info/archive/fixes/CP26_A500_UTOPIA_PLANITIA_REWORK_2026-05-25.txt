CP26 - Arc A500 / Utopia Planitia rework
Date: 2026-05-25

Summary
- Reworked the A500 Mars arc to start its journal entry from the first popup instead of only at the open rebellion stage.
- Replaced the old hidden 30-day delayed trigger with three visible special-project branches:
  - military ship response
  - science ship response
  - governor ship response
- Added a new Federation quest-tree node between Enterprise upgrade and Odyssey:
  - Modernisation d'Utopia Planitia / Modernisation of Utopia Planitia
  - requires 3 Federation colonies with A500 deployments
  - unlocks Odyssey research access on completion

Gameplay changes
- `cp26_a500.1` still exists and remains the entry popup for the arc.
- The three first responses now map to ship classes:
  - security -> military ship
  - technician -> science ship
  - administrative oversight -> governor ship
- The mission chain uses the existing A500 DDS but now appears earlier and remains active until resolution.
- Military response still leads to an armed insurrection path, but with reduced rebel strength.
- Science response can still resolve the crisis peacefully.
- Odyssey is no longer handed out directly by the A500 military resolution; it is gated behind the new mission-tree step.
