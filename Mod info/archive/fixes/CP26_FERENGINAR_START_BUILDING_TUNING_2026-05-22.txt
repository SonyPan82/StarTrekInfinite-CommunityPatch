CP26 - Ferenginar start building tuning
Date: 2026-05-22

Requested start state
- Present by default on Ferenginar:
  - Bourse du Grand Nagus
  - Guilde des marchands alimentaires
- Not present by default:
  - Consortium minier du DaiMon
- No free early unlock of the energy/mining expansion techs.

Applied changes
- create_ferenginar_colony now adds only:
  - cp26_ferengi_energy_consortium
  - cp26_ferengi_food_consortium
- aa_ferengi_initializer no longer pre-places cp26_ferengi_mining_consortium.
- cp26_setlik.4 no longer grants:
  - tech_power_hub_1
  - tech_mineral_purification_1

Gameplay result
- Ferenginar starts with the two intended thematic buildings.
- Mining consortium must be unlocked normally through research.
- Grand Nagus Exchange can exist on Ferenginar at start without making the wider tech line free immediately.
