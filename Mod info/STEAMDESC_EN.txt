[h1]COMMUNITY PATCH 2026 - Star Trek: Infinite[/h1]

An actively maintained community patch for Star Trek: Infinite. Merged mods, crash fixes, rebalancing, and new narrative content ; all in one clean, stable, continuously updated package.

[i]Star Trek: Infinite deserved better. We're on it.[/i]


[h2]NEW CONTENT — A500 ARC (TESTING)[/h2]

[b]Utopia Planitia Rebellion[/b] — UFOP narrative arc inspired by Star Trek: Picard

[list]
[*] New technology: [b]A500 Synthetic Androids[/b] — deployable on your colonies as industrial workers
[*] New planetary decision: [b]Deploy A500 Units[/b] — adds 3 synthetic pops and a production bonus
[*] If deployed on [b]Mars[/b]: the Rebellion arc triggers after 5 in-game years
[*] [b]Two resolution paths[/b]: military (troop transport + ground combat) or diplomatic (science vessel)
[*] Rewards: Neo-Constitution, Sagan, Duderstadt and Odyssey ship designs added to the research pool
[*] [b]Mission log[/b]: the arc is tracked in the situation log until resolution
[*] [b]Post-arc management[/b]: new [i]Synthetic Worker[/i] species profile (0 housing, 0 amenities, migration locked) + [i]Ban Synthetic Workers[/i] edict (permanent purge, irreversible)
[*] Can be disabled in the game-start popup
[/list]

[b]Romulan Objective — Synth Project[/b]

[list]
[*] When the Federation activates A500 units, a 90-day window opens to send a spy ship to Earth
[*] Successful mission → unlocks a national decision: trigger a synthetic rebellion on an enemy planet (not capitals)
[/list]
