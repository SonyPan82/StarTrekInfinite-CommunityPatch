CP26 Dominion War Design
Star Trek: Infinite - Community Patch 2026

Purpose
-------
Create a major dynamic Dominion War arc for Star Trek: Infinite.

The goal is not to force the exact Deep Space Nine timeline every game, but to
build a living strategic crisis around the Bajoran Wormhole, Dominion
infiltration, Cardassian instability, Deep Space 9, the Federation Alliance,
the Romulan entry into the war, the Breen intervention, and the final fate of
Cardassia.

This should become one of CP26's major narrative systems, alongside the
Synthetic Crisis arc.


Core Fantasy
------------
The Dominion War should feel like the Alpha Quadrant being slowly pulled into a
conflict it does not fully understand until it is almost too late.

Early game:
    Rumors, exploration, Gamma Quadrant signals, diplomatic pressure,
    unexplained incidents, changeling infiltration.

Mid game:
    Cardassia weakens, the Dominion offers protection, DS9 and the wormhole
    become the center of strategic tension.

War phase:
    The Dominion and Cardassians push into the Alpha Quadrant. The Federation
    must build an alliance, hold the wormhole, and survive.

Late war:
    Romulans may enter, Breen may join the Dominion, Cardassian resistance may
    rise, and the war can end in surrender, devastation, negotiated peace, or
    Dominion victory.


Main System Variables
---------------------
cp26_dominion_tension
    General Alpha Quadrant tension with the Dominion.
    Rises through border incidents, Dominion convoys, wormhole disputes,
    Federation militarization, and Cardassian alignment.

cp26_changeling_infiltration
    Represents Founder agents operating inside Alpha Quadrant governments.
    High infiltration can cause false-flag events, diplomatic breakdown,
    paranoia, coups, or bad strategic choices.

cp26_wormhole_control
    Represents strategic control over the Bajoran Wormhole / DS9.
    Positive values favor the Federation Alliance.
    Negative values favor the Dominion.

cp26_cardassian_instability
    Represents Cardassian weakness, political frustration, military collapse,
    and willingness to accept Dominion protection.

cp26_alpha_quadrant_readiness
    Represents Federation/Klingon/Romulan preparation for full-scale war.
    High readiness improves outcomes in DS9, Operation Return, and the final
    war phase.

cp26_dominion_foothold
    Represents Dominion military infrastructure in the Alpha Quadrant.
    Increases if wormhole convoys are ignored or Cardassia joins the Dominion.

cp26_cardassian_resistance
    Represents Damar-style resistance against Dominion rule.
    Rises if Dominion pressure on Cardassia becomes brutal or if Allied
    support reaches Cardassian dissidents.

cp26_breen_threat
    Represents the chance and strength of Breen intervention.
    Increases during late war if the Dominion is losing or if diplomatic
    isolation pushes the Breen toward the Dominion.


Phase 1 - Dominion Cold War
---------------------------
This phase begins before open war.

Possible triggers:

- Bajoran Wormhole exists
- Federation has contact with Bajor / DS9 area
- Cardassia exists
- Gamma Quadrant/Dominion content has been initialized
- Year/progression threshold if needed

Event themes:

1. Gamma Quadrant Rumors
    Traders and explorers report a power called the Dominion.

2. Missing Ships
    Federation, Klingon, or Cardassian ships vanish near the Gamma Quadrant.

3. Jem'Hadar Warning
    A direct warning tells Alpha Quadrant powers to stay out of Dominion space.

4. Changeling Fear
    Reports of shapeshifter infiltration create political paranoia.

5. Antwerp-Style Security Panic
    A terrorist incident or false flag raises security pressure inside the
    Federation.

6. Klingon-Cardassian Tension
    Klingons suspect Cardassia has been compromised by the Founders.

7. Federation Mediation
    The Federation can support Cardassia diplomatically, side with Klingons,
    or attempt neutrality.

8. Obsidian Order Collapse / Cardassian Weakness
    Cardassia becomes politically unstable and vulnerable to Dominion influence.

Gameplay effects:

- Tension rises gradually.
- Infiltration can push powers toward bad wars.
- Federation can invest in readiness or diplomacy.
- Cardassia can be saved, isolated, or pushed into Dominion arms.


Phase 2 - Bajor, DS9, And The Wormhole
--------------------------------------
The wormhole should be the center of the Dominion War system.

Strategic states:

Federation-controlled DS9
    Federation detects Dominion movement earlier.
    Can mine the wormhole.
    Can launch Operation Return if station is lost.

Neutral / Bajoran-controlled DS9
    Lower military readiness.
    More diplomatic options.
    Higher risk if Bajor signs non-aggression pact.

Cardassian / Dominion-controlled DS9
    Dominion foothold grows.
    Wormhole convoys can arrive.
    Federation Alliance must plan recapture.

Possible mechanics:

- Decision: Fortify Deep Space 9
- Decision: Mine The Wormhole
- Special project: Self-Replicating Minefield
- Modifier: Wormhole Blockaded
- Event: Dominion Convoys Enter The Alpha Quadrant
- Event: Bajor Signs Non-Aggression Pact

Minefield effects:

If active:
    Dominion reinforcements from Gamma Quadrant are blocked.
    Dominion tension rises sharply.
    Open war becomes likely.

If destroyed or absent:
    Dominion foothold increases.
    Jem'Hadar fleets can spawn in or near Cardassian/Dominion space.


Phase 3 - Cardassia Joins The Dominion
--------------------------------------
Cardassia is the hinge of the war.

Trigger conditions:

- cp26_cardassian_instability high
- Cardassia weakened by Klingons, Federation, Maquis, or internal collapse
- Dominion tension high
- Dominion contact established

Major event:

cp26_dominion.300 - Cardassia Seeks Protection

Cardassian choices:

1. Join the Dominion
    Cardassia gains massive military support.
    Jem'Hadar fleets appear.
    Dominion foothold increases greatly.
    Federation/Klingon alliance likely reforms.

2. Reject the Dominion
    Cardassia remains independent.
    Internal instability continues.
    Dominion may attempt infiltration or coup.

3. Seek Federation mediation
    Opens non-canon diplomatic path.
    Can prevent full Dominion War but weakens Cardassian militarists.

4. Military hardline
    Cardassia tries to stand alone.
    High risk of collapse or later forced Dominion alliance.

If Cardassia joins Dominion:

- Dukat-style leadership can appear as event flavor.
- Cardassian fleets receive Dominion support modifiers.
- Maquis/DMZ content can be suppressed.
- Dominion War countdown begins.


Phase 4 - Call To Arms
----------------------
The open war begins around DS9 and the wormhole.

Possible triggers:

- Federation mines the wormhole
- Dominion demands removal of the minefield
- Dominion attacks DS9
- Dominion foothold high
- Cardassia joined Dominion
- Dominion convoys ignored for too long

Major event:

cp26_dominion.400 - Call To Arms

Federation choices:

1. Mine the wormhole
    Blocks reinforcements.
    Guarantees Dominion hostility.
    Improves long-term war odds if DS9 survives or is recaptured.

2. Reinforce DS9
    Increases chance DS9 holds.
    Costs resources and fleet readiness.

3. Evacuate and prepare counterattack
    DS9 likely falls.
    Operation Return becomes available.
    Lower immediate casualties.

4. Seek diplomatic delay
    Reduces immediate war risk.
    Dominion foothold may increase.

Possible outcomes:

- DS9 holds
- DS9 falls but minefield activates
- DS9 falls and wormhole remains open
- Dominion foothold surges


Phase 5 - Operation Return
--------------------------
If DS9 falls, the Federation Alliance can attempt to recapture it.

Special project / situation:

cp26_operation_return

Requirements:

- DS9 lost to Dominion/Cardassia
- Federation still active
- Klingons allied or available
- Enough readiness or fleet strength

Inputs:

- cp26_alpha_quadrant_readiness
- cp26_wormhole_control
- Klingon support
- Dominion foothold
- Minefield status
- Changeling infiltration

Outcomes:

1. Decisive Allied Victory
    DS9 recaptured.
    Dominion retreats to Cardassian space.
    Wormhole control shifts to Federation.
    Dominion foothold reduced.

2. Costly Victory
    DS9 recaptured but fleets suffer losses.
    Federation war exhaustion rises.
    Dominion remains dangerous.

3. Failed Offensive
    DS9 remains occupied.
    Dominion reinforcements increase.
    Federation Alliance morale drops.

4. Prophets Intervention / Wormhole Event
    Optional rare/canon-inspired outcome.
    Gamma Quadrant reinforcements disappear.
    Strong narrative event, should be used carefully.


Phase 6 - Federation Alliance
-----------------------------
The Federation should not win alone easily.

Alliance members:

- Federation
- Klingon Empire
- Romulan Star Empire later
- Cardassian rebels later
- Minor powers optionally

Klingon support:

- May be weakened if Federation-Klingon relations were damaged earlier.
- Can arrive during Operation Return.
- Can become essential after Breen weapon appears.

Romulan support:

Romulans begin neutral or with non-aggression pact.

Event chain:

cp26_dominion.600 - Romulan Neutrality
cp26_dominion.610 - Evidence Against The Dominion
cp26_dominion.620 - Romulus Enters The War

Possible routes:

1. Honest diplomacy
    Harder, slower, lower scandal risk.

2. Intelligence operation
    Faster, higher risk.
    Inspired by In the Pale Moonlight.

3. Shared threat
    Romulans join if Dominion foothold gets too high.

4. Failure
    Romulans stay neutral or hostile.


Phase 7 - Breen Intervention
----------------------------
Late-war escalation.

Trigger conditions:

- Dominion losing or stalled
- War has lasted long enough
- Breen exist
- cp26_breen_threat high

Major event:

cp26_dominion.700 - The Breen Join The Dominion

Effects:

- Breen-Dominion Alliance formed
- Allied fleets receive temporary combat penalty
- Breen energy dampener modifier active
- Chin'toka-style defeat event possible

Modifier:

modifier_cp26_breen_energy_dampener
    Federation and Romulan ship combat penalty
    Klingon ships less affected or recover faster

Countermeasure project:

cp26_breen_weapon_countermeasure

Outcomes:

- Klingon adaptation discovered quickly
- Federation/Romulan countermeasure developed later
- If ignored, Dominion counteroffensive becomes devastating


Phase 8 - Cardassian Rebellion
------------------------------
If Dominion control over Cardassia becomes brutal, resistance rises.

Variable:

cp26_cardassian_resistance

Increases when:

- Dominion sacrifices Cardassian fleets
- Cardassian worlds suffer devastation
- Damar-style events occur
- Federation supports dissidents
- Dominion loses trust

Major events:

cp26_dominion.800 - Cardassian Discontent
cp26_dominion.810 - Damar's Rebellion
cp26_dominion.820 - Cardassian Liberation Front

Effects:

- Dominion shipbuilding disrupted
- Cardassian fleets may defect
- Dominion foothold decreases
- Final attack on Cardassia becomes possible

Player choices:

- Arm the rebels
- Protect civilians
- Use rebellion as military diversion
- Promise post-war Cardassian sovereignty
- Ignore them and focus on military victory


Phase 9 - Battle Of Cardassia
-----------------------------
Final phase of the war.

Trigger conditions:

- Dominion weakened
- Federation Alliance active
- Cardassian resistance active or Dominion foothold low
- Breen countermeasure developed or Breen threat contained

Major event:

cp26_dominion.900 - The Battle Of Cardassia

Inputs:

- Federation readiness
- Klingon support
- Romulan support
- Cardassian resistance
- Dominion foothold
- Breen threat
- War exhaustion

Outcomes:

1. Dominion Surrender
    War ends.
    Dominion leaves Alpha Quadrant.
    Peace treaty signed at DS9 or equivalent.

2. Pyrrhic Allied Victory
    Cardassia devastated.
    Heavy civilian casualties.
    Federation Alliance wins but suffers long-term trauma.

3. Negotiated Settlement
    Dominion withdraws but remains intact.
    Cardassia survives with reduced devastation.
    Less decisive but more stable.

4. Dominion Holds Cardassia
    War continues or ends in cold peace.
    Alpha Quadrant remains militarized.

5. Dominion Victory
    Rare failure outcome.
    DS9/wormhole lost.
    Federation Alliance collapses or accepts harsh peace.


Post-War Effects
----------------
Federation victory:

- Unity and diplomatic bonus
- War exhaustion recovery period
- Ship losses / rebuilding need
- Stronger alliance with Klingons and Romulans if they joined

Cardassia devastated:

- Cardassian rebuilding event chain
- Refugees and humanitarian aid
- Damar legacy / Cardassian reform

Dominion withdrawal:

- Wormhole restricted
- Dominion no longer spawns reinforcements
- Possible later diplomatic contact with the Great Link

Federation militarized:

- Stronger fleets
- Lower exploration/diplomatic focus
- Potential connection to later Picard-era defensive Starfleet


Integration With CP26 Content
-----------------------------
Added civilizations:

Breen Confederacy
    Can join the Dominion late-war.
    Should not be forced every game, but should have a strong event path.

Gorn Hegemony
    Could remain neutral, opportunistic, or provide limited support.

Orion Empire
    Can be involved through smuggling, intelligence, or Dominion plots.

Tholian Assembly
    Can sign non-aggression pact with Dominion or stay isolationist.

Remans / Romulans
    Remans can be referenced if Romulans join the war.
    Shinzon/Reman assault troops can be optional later flavor.

Mars/Luna/Utopia Planitia
    Dominion War shipbuilding demands can interact with Mars industry.
    Later Synthetic Crisis can be made more likely if wartime production pressure
    pushes the Federation toward synthetic labor.

Synthetic Crisis connection:

If Dominion War caused heavy losses:
    Synthetic Labor Program should become more attractive.
    Federation may seek industrial shortcuts.
    This creates a natural bridge from DS9-era trauma to Picard-era Mars.

No Pirates option:
    No direct connection needed.

Galaxy size:
    Large map helps Dominion War feel larger, but fleet spawns must be careful
    to avoid performance crashes.

- Cardassian instability variable
- Wormhole control variable

- Call to Arms event

Design Principles
-----------------
1. The war should build gradually.
    The player should feel the Alpha Quadrant sliding toward disaster.

2. DS9 and the wormhole must matter.
    Whoever controls the wormhole controls the pace of the war.

3. Cardassia should not be a simple villain switch.
    Cardassian instability, pride, fear, and survival should drive the alliance
    with the Dominion.

4. Diplomacy should matter.
    Klingon, Romulan, Bajoran, Breen, and minor power choices should change the
    course of the war.

5. Multiple endings should be valid.
    Canon victory, negotiated peace, Cardassian survival, Dominion foothold, and
    catastrophic defeat should all be possible.

6. Performance must be protected.
    Use modifiers, events, and limited fleets before adding massive battle
    spawns.

7. Connect to later CP26 arcs.
    A brutal Dominion War can naturally push the Federation toward the Synthetic
    Labor Program and the later Mars Crisis.


Open Questions
--------------
1. Does CP26 currently have a reliable DS9/Bajor/wormhole representation?

2. Should the Dominion exist as a full playable/AI country, or as scripted
   crisis fleets and event targets in version 1?

3. Should Cardassia be allowed to avoid joining the Dominion?

4. Should Bajor be able to sign a non-aggression pact and stay out of the war?

5. Should Operation Return include a rare Prophets intervention outcome?

6. Should the Breen always appear late-war, or only if the Dominion is losing?

7. How much should the war affect post-war Federation ethics and shipbuilding?


Recommended First Implementation
--------------------------------
Start with a safe, event-driven version:

1. Add variables and startup initialization.
2. Add cold war events that raise tension and infiltration.
3. Add Cardassian instability and Dominion alliance event.
4. Add wormhole control as a variable, not map surgery at first.
5. Add Call to Arms and Operation Return as events/projects.
6. Add Romulan and Breen event branches.
7. Add Cardassian Rebellion and Battle of Cardassia outcome.
8. Only after stability testing, add more visible fleets, map changes, and AI
   behaviors.

This gives CP26 a large Dominion War narrative without risking crashes from too
many scripted fleet spawns too early.
