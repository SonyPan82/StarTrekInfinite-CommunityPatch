CP26 FIX NOTE
Date: 2026-05-26
Topic: Visible secondary hull branches for Klingon, Romulan, and Cardassian factions

Summary
- Added one lore-friendly visible side branch for each non-UFOP major faction that still felt under-served compared to Federation content:
  - Klingon: Jach battlecruiser
  - Romulan: Praetor warbird
  - Cardassian: Hutet dreadnought

Implementation
- New faction-locked engineering technologies:
  - tech_cp26_klingon_jach_unlock
  - tech_cp26_romulan_praetor_unlock
  - tech_cp26_cardassian_hutet_unlock
- New custom ship sizes, section templates, event designs, and imported shipset assets were added.

Tech tree placement
- Jach branches after the Klingon late military line and requires both battleship access and the Kamarag branch.
- Praetor branches after the D'deridex era and requires artificial singularities tier 3.
- Hutet branches after the Cardassian battleship tier as a heavy visible prestige hull below Nokoram.

Deliberate exclusions
- Scimitar was not added in this pass. It remains reserved for a future mission-tree or narrative branch.
- No D7, T'liss, or extra NSC-role hulls were added in this pass to avoid bloating the deck.
