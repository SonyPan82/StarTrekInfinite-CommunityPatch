CP26 - Ferengi Beta name hardcode fix
Date: 2026-05-21

Issue:
- The Ferengi empire name still appeared as `Alliance Ferengi` in the empire-selection card,
  even after the localisation key `EMPIRE_DESIGN_FERENGI` had been changed to
  `Alliance Ferengi (Bêta)` / `Ferengi Alliance (Bêta)`.

Cause:
- The visible name was still hardcoded in two gameplay sources:
  - `prescripted_countries/_04_ferengi.txt`
  - `common/solar_system_initializers/aa_ferengi_initializer.txt`
- Those hardcoded values overrode or bypassed the updated localisation key in practice.

Fix:
- Replaced the hardcoded Ferengi empire name with:
  - `EMPIRE_DESIGN_FERENGI`

Files changed:
- `prescripted_countries/_04_ferengi.txt`
- `common/solar_system_initializers/aa_ferengi_initializer.txt`
