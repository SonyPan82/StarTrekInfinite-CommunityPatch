CP26 AI EXPANSION AUDIT AND START BUFF - 2026-06-07

Context
- Audit requested after reports that Cardassian, Ferengi, and other empires seemed to expand weakly.
- Vanilla and CP26 files were compared.

Key finding
- Dilithium is not the direct expansion blocker.
- Vanilla AI expansion is mainly gated by alloys and influence.
- CP26 starts were checked against that requirement.

What vanilla uses for expansion
- Alloys budget for starbase expansion.
- Influence budget for stations.
- Dilithium is budgeted for ships, planets, upgrades, and trade, not for territorial outpost expansion itself.

Audit summary
- UFOP: comfortable start, no expansion help needed.
- Romulans: solid territorial start, no clear expansion seed issue.
- Tholians: already well served.
- Gorn: AI enabled and structurally fine on paper.
- Orion: AI enabled and structurally fine on paper.
- Klingons: under-seeded for expansion because no specific alloys/influence cushion.
- Cardassians: most clearly under-seeded for expansion; no alloys and no influence cushion.
- Ferengi: has influence, but no alloy cushion; commerce-friendly start, weak expansion push.
- Small one-system minors: generally not seeded as aggressive expanders, which appears intentional.

Decision
- Apply a minimal AI-only start buff.
- Do not touch dilithium.
- Do not globally rewrite vanilla AI budgets.
- Do not buff every empire equally.

Implemented AI-only start buffs
- Klingon Empire
  - +150 alloys
  - +50 influence
- Cardassian Union
  - +200 alloys
  - +75 influence
- Ferengi Alliance
  - +200 alloys

Why this version
- It directly supports the two real expansion resources the vanilla AI uses.
- It avoids inflating player starts.
- It keeps UFOP/Romulans/Tholians unchanged.
- It leaves Gorn/Orion untouched for now because their AI setup appears structurally valid.

What to observe in testing
- First 20 to 40 years.
- Whether Cardassians claim nearby systems more consistently.
- Whether Ferengi leave home and establish a wider footprint.
- Whether Klingons stop lagging behind despite available free space.
- Whether global expansion now feels healthier without all empires snowballing too hard.

If still too weak after testing
- Next likely step would be a second very small AI-only pass on:
  - Klingons
  - Cardassians
  - Ferengi
- Not a dilithium fix.
- Not a blanket buff for every empire.
