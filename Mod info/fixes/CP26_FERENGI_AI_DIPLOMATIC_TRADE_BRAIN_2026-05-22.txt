CP26 Ferengi AI Diplomatic Trade Brain
Date: 2026-05-22

Summary
- Strengthened Ferengi AI diplomacy so their strategic goal aligns better with
  commercial pacts and profitable platform expansion.

Changes
1. New dedicated Ferengi AI personality:
   - File: common/personalities/aa_ferengi_personalities.txt
   - Key: cp26_grand_nagus_merchants
   - Effects:
     - very high trade willingness
     - very high commercial pact acceptance
     - stronger preference for non-aggression and stable markets
     - lower war appetite than baseline major empires

2. Commercial pact threshold relaxed when a Ferengi is involved:
   - File: common/diplomatic_actions/00_actions.txt
   - Rule:
     - normal empires still require good-or-better opinion
     - Ferengi-involved deals can pass at neutral opinion
     - improving relations still works as before

3. Ferengi AI host selection improved:
   - File: events/cp26_ferengi_branch_events.txt
   - AI now prefers larger host markets first:
     - first try: 8+ owned planets
     - fallback: 5+ owned planets
     - fallback: any otherwise valid commercial partner

Intent
- Make Ferengi AI more likely to:
  - seek and keep commercial pacts
  - prioritize worthwhile markets
  - better leverage its unique megastructure/platform economy

Notes
- Construction logic remains the existing monthly backend.
- No player-facing Ferengi UI or workflow was changed in this pass.
