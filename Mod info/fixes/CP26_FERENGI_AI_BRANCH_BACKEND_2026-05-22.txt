CP26 - Ferengi AI branch-office backend
Date: 2026-05-22

1. Goal
- Give AI-controlled Ferengi empires an autonomous monthly backend to place custom CP26 branch offices
  without relying on the player-only UI / edict flow.

2. Implementation
- Added a second monthly Ferengi branch pulse:
  - `cp26_ferengi_branch.101`
- Hooked through:
  - `common/on_actions/cp26_ferengi_branch_on_actions.txt`

3. Eligibility
- The AI branch event only runs when the country:
  - is AI-controlled
  - is a Ferengi empire
  - is not on cooldown
  - has at least 50 influence
  - has at least 400 minerals
- It also requires at least one valid foreign target empire with:
  - active commercial pact
  - non-corporate authority
  - not already hosting one of this Ferengi empire's branch relations
  - at least one colony with upgraded capital
  - no existing CP26 Ferengi branch modifier on that colony

4. Selection logic
- Each attempt:
  - picks one random valid host empire
  - picks one random eligible colony
  - places one branch
- Branch choice:
  - if the AI has at least 500 minerals:
    - 45% Bureau du Grand Nagus
    - 25% Équipe d'Acquisition
    - 30% Bar de Quark
  - otherwise:
    - defaults to Bureau du Grand Nagus

5. Costs
- Bureau du Grand Nagus:
  - 50 influence
  - 400 minerals
- Équipe d'Acquisition:
  - 50 influence
  - 500 minerals
- Bar de Quark:
  - 50 influence
  - 500 minerals

6. Cadence and CPU policy
- AI branch placement uses a timed country flag cooldown:
  - `cp26_ferengi_branch_ai_cooldown`
  - 360 days
- Result:
  - branch expansion attempts happen at most once per year per AI Ferengi empire
  - the monthly pulse exits early if no valid target or insufficient resources
- This keeps the backend lightweight and avoids branch spam.

7. Revenue backend alignment
- Mining branch income modifiers were aligned to use:
  - `country_minerals_produces_add`
- This matches the broader top-bar-friendly income refactor approach already used elsewhere.

8. Limits
- Current AI behavior is intentionally simple and safe:
  - one branch relation per target empire
  - one attempt per yearly cooldown window
  - no strategic ranking by planet value yet
- This is designed as a stable release-grade backend, not a fully optimized grand-strategy planner.
