CP26 - Federation / Klingon Mutual Colony Visibility
Date: 2026-05-21

Problem
- Bespoke colonies and outpost systems were spawning, but visibility was asymmetric.
- Example:
  - when playing the Federation, Rura Penthe / Beta Penthe appeared hidden
  - when playing Klingons, Vega and similar Federation-added colonies appeared hidden
- The issue was not spawn reliability, but startup survey visibility.

Fix
- Added targeted mutual survey visibility in:
  - events/game_start.txt -> game_start.1

Federation-added systems now revealed to Klingons
- Vega IX (`vega_ix_planet`)
- Denobula (`planet_denobula`)
- Risa (`planet_risa`)
- Sol Nova / Terra Nova (`planet_terra_nova`)

Klingon-added systems now revealed to the Federation
- O'gat (`planet_ogat`)
- Narendra (`planet_narendra`)
- Boreth (`planet_boreth`)
- Khitomer (`planet_khitomer`)
- Rura Penthe (`planet_rura_penthe`)

Implementation notes
- The patch reveals only the relevant bespoke systems.
- It does not broadly reveal all Klingon or Federation space.
- It keeps the fog-of-war behavior mostly intact while making CP26-added colony content readable from both starts.
