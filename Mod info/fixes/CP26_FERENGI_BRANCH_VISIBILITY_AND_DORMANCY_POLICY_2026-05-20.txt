CP26 Ferengi Branches - Visibility and Dormancy Policy
Date: 2026-05-20

Summary
- Commercial pact visibility was changed from a monthly global reveal to a targeted reveal.
- Dormancy remains delayed purge, with a timed dormant modifier on the host planet.

What changed
1. Targeted host-planet reveal
- Removed the monthly reveal sweep from:
  events/cp26_ferengi_branch_events.txt
- Added one-shot reveal of all host colonies when:
  - a Ferengi player opens the branch manager on an eligible foreign planet
  - a Ferengi player successfully creates a branch office
- Implemented in:
  common/button_effects/cp26_button_effects.txt

2. Dormancy display
- Dormant branches use the timed planet modifier:
  cp26_ferengi_branch_dormant
- The countdown is therefore handled natively by the game on the planet modifier tooltip.
- This avoids using a fake decision entry that looks player-clickable from the host side.

Design rationale
- The monthly reveal was functional but heavier than needed.
- The targeted reveal gives Ferengi players practical visibility exactly when branch interaction starts.
- Host-side dormancy remains readable without exposing a misleading planet decision.
