CP26 - FERENGI EMPIRE / SHIPSET AUDIT
Date: 2026-05-17

1) Country type / diplomacy

- The Ferengi were moved off `contender` and into a dedicated country type:
  `ferengi_alliance`
- Reason:
  `contender` made them too visible too early and did not match the intended
  first-contact behavior.
- Side effect discovered:
  many diplomatic actions in vanilla / Hornblower / CP26 were explicitly gated
  to `default` or `contender`.
- Result:
  the Ferengi existed correctly, but several diplomatic actions were missing in
  the diplomacy view because `ferengi_alliance` was not included in those
  filters.

Fix applied:
- `ferengi_alliance` added to regular-empire gating in:
  - common/scripted_triggers/z_aa_scripted_triggers.txt
  - common/diplomatic_actions/00_actions.txt
  - common/diplomatic_actions/00_hornblower_actions.txt
  - events/zz_cp26_war_negotiation_events.txt
  - events/zz_cp26_ai_shipyard_fix.txt

Conclusion:
- Ferengi remain hidden before proper contact
- Ferengi are now treated as a regular diplomatic empire where the mod/UI was
  previously filtering them out
- This is cleaner than forcing them back into `contender`


2) Lore-friendly species traits

Current intended Ferengi species setup:
- trait_thrifty
- trait_eye_for_business_ferengi
- trait_traditionalists_ferengi

Reasoning:
- `trait_thrifty` is the closest vanilla baseline for Ferengi economic instinct
- `trait_eye_for_business_ferengi` reinforces profit-seeking and specialist
  economic competence
- `trait_traditionalists_ferengi` reflects a society shaped by the Rules of
  Acquisition and a narrower cultural relationship to open-ended research

Important note:
- existing saves may keep older trait states on already-generated Ferengi pops
- the clean validation point for species traits is a new game


3) Shipset audit

Observed in-game symptoms:
- magenta / pink blocks and strange panels on some Ferengi ships
- animation warnings in error.log (`Could not find animation "idle"`)

Confirmed findings:
- Ferengi frame animation was referencing `generic_01_corvette_frame_idle_animation`
  instead of the Ferengi animation asset actually present in the imported pack
- the Ferengi animation pack only defines:
  - ferengi_01_corvette_frame_idle_animation
  - ferengi_01_corvette_frame_moving_animation
  - ferengi_01_corvette_frame_death_animation
  - ferengi_01_corvette_frame_death2_animation
  - ferengi_01_corvette_frame_death3_animation

Fix applied:
- `gfx/models/ships/ferengi_01/ferengi_01_corvette_frame.gfx`
  now uses:
  - idle   -> ferengi_01_corvette_frame_idle_animation
  - attack -> ferengi_01_corvette_frame_idle_animation
  - moving -> ferengi_01_corvette_frame_moving_animation

Status after this pass:
