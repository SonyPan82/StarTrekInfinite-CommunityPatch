[h1]Community Patch 2026 - Version 1.7.3[/h1]

[h2]Technology and Ship Progression[/h2]

[list]
[*] Reworked the major faction technology flow into a more coherent, lore-friendly progression.
[*] Federation, Klingon, Romulan, Cardassian, and Ferengi ship unlock paths now follow a clearer chronological order instead of random or immersion-breaking jumps.
[*] Major factions now start with technologies that better match their 2346 situation, doctrine, and lore identity.
[*] Several ship technologies were restructured into a more controlled progression, so earlier hulls unlock before later prestige classes.
[*] Romulans and Klingons now follow a more lore-friendly cloaking progression, with both factions properly tied to cloaking technology and distinct military identity.
[*] Ferengi progression has been expanded with new military ships: Nandi, Galin, and Ooron.
[*] Klingons and Romulans received new late-game visible ship branches: Jach and Praetor.
[*] The Romulan Scimitar has been integrated as a special late-game unlock tied to the mission tree and alloy investment.
[*] Multiple unlock conditions, prerequisites, and ship-related technologies were updated to better fit faction flavor and era progression.
