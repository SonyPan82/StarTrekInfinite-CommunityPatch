[h1]Community Patch 2026 - Version 1.6.2[/h1]

Hello, hello!

I’m happy to release version 1.6.2 of Community Patch 2026!

This update was not made without a few Cardassian tortures… but it is finally here!

[h2]Ferengi Alliance[/h2]

[list]
[*] The Ferengi Alliance is now a fully playable empire.
[*] Added exclusive commercial bonuses.
[*] New unique planetary bonuses.
[*] New Ferengi AI focused on opportunism and profit rather than direct warfare.
[*] Ferengi can now construct commercial megastructures inside the systems of empires with active commercial pacts.
[*] Megastructures are unlocked through the technology tree.
[*] These structures provide bonuses to both the host empire and the Ferengi Alliance.
[*] Added new Ferengi-exclusive districts.
[/list]

[h2]Rework - Occupied States[/h2]

[list]
[*] The vassalization system has been reworked to better integrate with existing game mechanics.
[*] Empires can now demand the occupation of another empire.
[*] Refusing will generate a casus belli and may lead to war.
[*] Occupied States now transfer part of their resources to their overlord.
[*] This mechanic can still be disabled at game start for a more “vanilla” experience.
[/list]

[h2]Content[/h2]

[list]
[*] Reworked Federation and Klingon diplomatic vessels.
[*] Added the Nebula Class for the Federation.
[/list]

[h2]Fixes & Improvements[/h2]

[list]
[*] Various fixes applied to improve overall game stability.
[*] Improved in-game font readability on certain displays.
[*] Feedback regarding the new font is always welcome.
[/list]

[h2]Developer Note[/h2]

[list]
