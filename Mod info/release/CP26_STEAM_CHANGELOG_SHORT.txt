[h1]Community Patch 2026 - Version 1.7.5[/h1]

[h2]Factions[/h2]

[list]
[*] Gorn and Orion are now treated as major factions, with their own starting technologies.
[*] New shipsets for Gorn and Orion.
[*] Federation and Ferengi opening progression received additional balance cleanup.
[*] Large and late-game ship classes across multiple factions were rescaled and normalized for better visual consistency.
[*] Ferengi now have a mission tree, currently still under construction and testing.
[/list]

[h2]Borg Crisis[/h2]

[list]
[*] The Janeway / Voyager path is now properly tracked in the situation log. You can follow the three-step Voyager gambit (Probe Defenses → Counteract Measures → Deliverance) with a visible progress counter.
[*] The Freed — liberated drones who can become unexpected allies against the Collective — now have their own dedicated situation log chain, with proper title, description and progress tracking.
[*] All Borg crisis chains (Janeway path, highways path, The Freed) now close cleanly on both victory and defeat — no more dangling entries in your situation log.
[*] Added a safeguard to prevent the Janeway failure timer from firing multiple times in edge cases (e.g. death during a project). The 81-year delay before the final Borg assault is unchanged.
[/list]

[h2]Hero Ships[/h2]

[list]
[*] Voyager and the D'deridex Hero were practically unkillable — their hitpoints had been multiplied far beyond vanilla values. Both have been rebalanced to remain powerful flagship-class vessels without being invincible. Voyager goes from 7000 to 2500 HP, the D'deridex Hero from 10000 to 4500 HP.
[*] Hero ships are still stronger and more durable than their standard counterparts — but a determined fleet can now threaten them if left unsupported.
[/list]


As always, your feedback is extremely valuable for improving the project.
