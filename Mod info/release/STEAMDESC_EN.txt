[h1]COMMUNITY PATCH 2026 - Star Trek: Infinite[/h1]

Community Patch 2026 is an actively maintained community overhaul for [i]Star Trek: Infinite[/i].

It brings together a wide range of bug fixes, stability improvements, balance adjustments, and integrated content into a single package designed to make campaigns feel more coherent, more complete, and more enjoyable over time.

[h2]MAIN FEATURES[/h2]

[list]
[*] Integration or restoration of several factions, secondary empires, and additional colonies
[*] New ship classes, visual variants, and fleet-related content
[*] Restored, expanded, or reworked diplomatic options
[*] Additional campaign settings, and narrative arcs
[*] Fixes for galaxy generation, scripts, general stability, and many inconsistencies in the base game
[*] A range of balance changes aimed at improving pacing, clarity, and overall cohesion
[/list]

[h2]WHAT CP26 ADDS OVER VANILLA[/h2]

[list]
[*] New factions integrated into the galaxy, including Ferengi, Gorn, Orion, Tholian, and other secondary content
[*] Additional or restored colonies and systems for major powers and minor factions
[*] New ship classes, shipsets, global designs, and visual variants for several empires
[*] Expanded diplomacy with restored vassalization, subjugation wars, and additional interactions
[*] Reworked AI, personalities, campaign starts, and overall balance
[*] Extended events, narrative arcs, quest content, and game-start options
[*] Expanded music, interface, and presentation for a more complete campaign experience
[/list]

[h2]MOD GOAL[/h2]

The goal of CP26 is straightforward: to offer a more stable, more complete, and more Star Trek-faithful experience while staying close to the structure of the vanilla game and preserving as much of its identity as possible.

[h2]INSTALLATION[/h2]

[list]
[*] Enable [b]this mod only[/b]
[*] Do not enable included or integrated mods separately
[/list]
